This directory is meant for saving shapefiles needed for generation of map plots.
As written in the README.md instructions, the needed shapefiles can be found on the data portal of Republic of Slovenia, Ministry of Natural Resources and Spatial Planning, via the following link: https://ipi.eprostor.gov.si/jgp/data. After entering the portal, follow "Državni topografski sistem" -> "Državna pregledna karta" -> "Državna pregledna karta merila 250.000 - rastrski podatki" -> "Prenos podatkov" and download the zipfiles  "naselja in objekti.zip", "komunikacije.zip", and "hidrografija.zip". The zipfiles should be extracted to this directory ("shapes"). For detailed desription of the contents and the name codes of shapefile data, consult the "shapefile_codes_description_GURS.docx" file that is already present in the "shapes" directory.
